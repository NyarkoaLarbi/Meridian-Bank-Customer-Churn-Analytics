Correlation vs. Causation Notice

This dataset is a cross-sectional customer snapshot without timestamps. Observed patterns indicate statistical association, not standalone causal proof.

  • Complaints & Exit Timing: A filed complaint strongly co-occurs with churn, but a cross-sectional snapshot cannot distinguish whether an unresolved complaint caused the departure or was logged during the account closure process.
  • Product Count & Account Structure: Higher churn among 3–4 product holders may reflect bundled fee structures, legacy product migration, or uncompetitive rates rather than product quantity itself causing churn.
  • Balance & Geography Effects: Regional churn disparities can stem from local competitive offers, branch closures, or demographic mix rather than customer geography directly driving attrition.
